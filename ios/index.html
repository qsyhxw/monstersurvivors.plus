iOS FAQ

Is there a Monster Survivors iOS app?

No, Monster Survivors is not available as a native iOS app in the App Store. However, it runs perfectly in Safari as an HTML5 browser game, giving you the same great experience without needing to download anything.

Does it work offline on iOS?

After your first visit, Safari caches the game files, allowing limited offline play. For the best experience, we recommend playing with an internet connection.

What iOS devices are supported?

Monster Survivors works on iPhone 8 and newer, iPad Air and newer, running iOS 13 or later with Safari browser.

Is it free on iOS?

Yes! Monster Survivors is 100% free to play on all platforms including iOS. No in-app purchases, no subscriptions.
