Before You Contact Us

Check if your question is answered below:

Is this the official Monster Survivors website?

No, this is a fan-made guide website. Monster Survivors is developed by FreezeNova. We are not affiliated with or endorsed by the game developers.

I found a bug in the game. Can you fix it?

We cannot fix game bugs as we don't develop the game. Please report bugs directly to FreezeNova or through the platform where you play the game.

I lost my game progress. Can you help?

Game progress is stored by the game itself, not our website. We cannot recover lost progress. Try clearing your browser cache or contacting the game platform for support.

Can I contribute guides or content?

We welcome community contributions! Please email us at contact@monstersurvivors.plus with your content idea or draft, and we'll review it for potential publication.

How can I advertise on your site?

For advertising and sponsorship opportunities, please contact us at business@monstersurvivors.plus with details about your proposal.
