FAQ

Unblocked and Browser Play Questions

Can I play Monster Survivors unblocked at school or work?

Only where games are allowed by that network or device owner. If access is blocked, follow the policy, ask the administrator or play later on a personal device and connection.

Does the browser version require a download?

No. The Play Online page loads the browser game in the page. Avoid third-party pages that ask for launchers, extensions or installers.

Is this the same as the VOODOO App?

No. This FAQ is for the separate FreezeNova browser version. The App guide pages cover a different product and progression system.

How do fullscreen and saves work?

Use the fullscreen control on the game frame or browser when available. Browser progress may depend on local storage, so private browsing, cleared cache or public/managed computers can remove saves.

What should I do if loading fails?

Refresh the page, try a current browser and check whether the network allows game content. If the network blocks it, use an allowed connection instead of trying App build fixes.
