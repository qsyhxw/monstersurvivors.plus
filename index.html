What is Monster Survivors?

Monster Survivors is a free-to-play roguelike survival game developed by FreezeNova. Battle endless waves of monsters including aggressive crabs, eerie pumpkins, flesh-eating plants, and evil bees in a thrilling browser-based experience.

The game features dynamic roguelike gameplay where you choose your weapon at the start, collect experience to level up, and unlock powerful upgrades. With two unique maps (Dreamy Woods and Shade Palace) and intense boss battles that shrink the play area, every run presents new challenges.

Play directly in your browser on platforms like OnlineGames.io and other gaming sites. No download required, no registration needed, completely free!
