What is Monster Survivors?

Monster Survivors is an action-packed roguelite survival game where you battle endless waves of terrifying monsters in an ever-changing apocalyptic world. Inspired by the popular "survivors-like" genre, this game combines auto-attack combat with deep strategic decision-making.

Featuring dynamic gameplay with character progression, weapon evolution systems, and epic boss battles, each run presents unique challenges. Choose from multiple characters with distinct abilities, unlock powerful weapons, and build devastating synergies to survive as long as possible.

Available on multiple platforms: Download on iOS, Android, Nintendo Switch, PlayStation, and Xbox. Also playable for free in your browser on various gaming platforms.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about Monster Survivors

Monster Survivors is available on iOS (App Store), Android (Google Play), Nintendo Switch, PlayStation, Xbox, and can also be played for free in web browsers on platforms like OnlineGames.io and FreezeNova. The mobile version "Monster Survivors: Last Stand" is the most popular version with millions of downloads.

Read full download guide

For beginners, we recommend starting with the Steel Sword for close-range combat or the Wooden Wand for ranged magical attacks. Both weapons are easy to use and scale well into mid-game. As you progress, prioritize getting the Lightning Staff or Boomerang for excellent crowd control. Check our tier list for complete rankings.

View weapon tier list

Characters are unlocked by completing specific achievements and challenges. The Guardian, Archer, and Knight are available from the start. Additional characters like the Warlock and Assassin require you to reach certain stages, defeat specific bosses, or collect enough gold. Each character has unique starting weapons and passive abilities that change your playstyle.

View all characters

The mobile version (Monster Survivors: Last Stand) does have in-app purchases and optional ads for rewards, but the game is fully playable without spending money. The web browser versions are completely free with no microtransactions. While purchasing items can speed up progression, skilled players can succeed with free-to-play strategies by focusing on optimal builds and understanding game mechanics.

Read F2P strategy guide

A typical run in Monster Survivors lasts between 10-30 minutes depending on your skill level and build. Beginners might survive 5-10 minutes initially, while experienced players with optimized builds can push past 30 minutes to face the final boss waves. The game progressively increases in difficulty with stronger enemies appearing every few minutes, culminating in epic boss battles every 5 stages.
