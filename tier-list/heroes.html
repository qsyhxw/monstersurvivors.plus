How Are Heroes Ranked?

Many beginner players assume any character can survive indefinitely with good weapons. However, unique passive abilities and starting weapons make top-tier heroes drastically out-prepare scaling waves.

  • S-Tier (Core Progressors): Characters with scaling abilities (e.g., extra armor, slow auras) that remain relevant even against 1M+ health bosses.
  • A-Tier (Strong Starters): Solid base stats that make early progression easy, but they may lack a late-game specific safety net.
  • B-Tier (Specialists): Fantastic at niche gameplay (like speedruns or pure evasion) but mechanically disadvantaged in pure DPS checking.
  • C-Tier: Situational or fundamentally squishy heroes.

S-Tier Heroes (The Meta Masters)

S

Echo

Extra Armor Trait Plasma Synergy

Echo is universally regarded as the absolute strongest best hero in Monster Survivors for deep progression. Their innate ability provides a passive extra layer of armor, completely negating one fatal mistake per cooldown. Furthermore, they are optimized to carry legendary Plasma Guns efficiently.

Echo Playstyle Guide

Arcanix

Level 5 Slow Aura High Casting Rate

An absolute monster when dealing with massive swarms. Arcanix develops an intrinsic slowing aura at level 5. This crowd control frees up your weapon slots—you don't need Frostbite if the hero already slows everything around them.

A-Tier Heroes (Strong Starters)

A

Knight

Great Sustain +10% Speed Baseline

The quintessential aggressive starter. His innate +10% movement speed combined with the default Short Sword means you can easily outrun and kite the first 15 waves while gathering materials freely.

Mage

+15% AoE Radius ~20 Base Damage

A classic glass cannon. With roughly 70 base health, they are squishy, but the innate bonus to Area of Effect ranges makes weapons like the Holy Cross or explosive bombs far more potent than on other characters.

B-Tier & C-Tier Heroes (Specialists)

B/C

Balencio (B-Tier Survivalist)

Balencio heavily emphasizes movement-based defense. Their unique environmental interaction provides a level-up mechanic connected to parkour elements (like trampolines). Extremely fun, but they lack the raw DPS passives necessary to dispatch bullet-sponge bosses efficiently.

Miyu (C-Tier Potential)

While Miyu possesses interesting burst mechanics, the community has routinely found her stat growth somewhat underwhelming relative to the resource cost. Useful for specific mobility challenges but outclassed by Arcanix.

Does My Hero Matter If I Have Good Weapons?

Yes! As you push into endlessly scaling waves, Weapon damage eventually flatlines (even at Legendary levels). At that pinnacle, your survival hinges entirely on Hero-specific mechanics: Arcanix's Slow Aura or Echo's Armor barrier literally rewrite the rules of progression.
