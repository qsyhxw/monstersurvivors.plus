Build Tier List

SGENERAL PROGRESSION

Balancio Gunslinger

Pistol + Shotgun + Plasma Beam or Chain Lightning, backed by Attack Speed, Damage, Range and Speed.

Why S: Balancio's visible Swift and Gunslinger path supports movement and firing speed without demanding a narrow map condition.

ASPECIALISTS

Horde, Boss & Safe Builds

Each can outperform the S-tier general build when the map matches its crowd, focused-damage or survival job.

Why A: excellent at one problem, but less efficient when that problem is absent.

BFARMING ONLY

XP & Resource Build

Chain Lightning and Plasma Beam with XP Gain, Pickup Range, Luck and Speed on repeatable content.

Why B for pushing: economy stats lose value when a boss or wave is already stopping progression. It becomes S-tier for safe farming.

CNO ROLE PLAN

Redundant Loadout

Three crowd weapons with no boss answer, or several focused weapons with no way to control normal enemies.

Why C: strong individual cards cannot compensate for a missing loadout job.

Tier movement

What Changes a Tier?

Current rank

A developed A-tier weapon can beat a fresh S-tier unlock.

Map layout

Obstacles raise Speed and Range; dense maps raise coverage.

Failure point

Boss damage, crowd pressure and survival require different S-tier answers.

Hero synergy

A hero's current upgrade card can raise the matching weapon or stat.

Avoid tier-list traps

Common Ranking Mistakes

Treating the tier as a damage formula

Tier combines role coverage, safety and investment value. It is not a fixed DPS measurement.

Ignoring weapon level and rank

Persistent upgrades can outweigh a theoretical base-tier advantage.

Using one list on every map

Boss, horde, obstacle and farming stages change what deserves S tier.

Mixing App and browser rankings

The two games use different weapons, heroes and progression systems.

Version Note

Monster Survivors App Tier List FAQ

What is S tier for general progression?

Balancio Gunslinger, Plasma Beam, Chain Lightning, Shotgun, Damage and Attack Speed are the safest universal priorities. A specialist can move above them on the right map.

Who is the best hero?

Balancio is the safest named general choice because Swift and Gunslinger support broadly useful Speed and Attack Speed. A developed specialist may be better for a matching boss, horde or survival stage.

Can an A-tier weapon be better than S tier?

Yes. Current level, rank, unlocked bonuses and the missing role in the loadout can outweigh the default tier.
