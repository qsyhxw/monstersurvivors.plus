What to Know About the Current Meta

In FreezeNova's Monster Survivors, your success deeply relies on your starting hero and the weapons you acquire during your run. While skill and movement are crucial, some characters and gear completely outclass others when pushing for late-game waves and boss fights. Our tier list ranks entities based on raw damage output, survivability, and crowd-control utility.

Ranking Criteria

  • Damage Scaling: How well the hero/weapon performs against late-game bosses.
  • Survivability & Utility: Movement speed advantages, crowd control (like freezing), and ease of use.
  • Synergy: How well starting weapons naturally evolve and interact with core passive upgrades.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I unlock S-Tier heroes like Echo or Arcanix?

Heroes in Monster Survivors are typically unlocked by collecting Hero Shards during your runs or spending accumulated in-game gold. Focus on surviving longer to maximize resource gains.

Are D-tier and C-tier weapons completely useless?

Not necessarily. While they fall off heavily in late-game boss fights, situational weapons like the Shuriken can still provide useful knockback in the first 5 minutes. However, we advise against investing heavy upgrades into them.
