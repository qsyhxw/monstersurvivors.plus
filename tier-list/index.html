Why Did We Split The Tier Lists?

As FreezeNova's Monster Survivors meta has deepened in late 2025 and 2026, comparing heroes directly against weapons or lumping them into a single massive chart causes immense confusion.

An S-Tier Hero might natively carry an S-Tier Weapon, but understanding why a hero’s passive makes them great is profoundly different from analyzing why a weapon's hit-box is effective. Thus, we have built targeted tier hubs to allow you to study exactly the data you need.
