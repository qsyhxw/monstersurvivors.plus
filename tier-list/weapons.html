How We Rank the Weapon Tier List

This is an editorial ranking for the VOODOO Monster Survivors App, not a fixed DPS claim. Each placement uses the same 100-point framework so the list can be compared across general progression and high-wave play.

  • S-Tier (Meta Defining): Leads across several scoring categories and remains reliable when waves become dense.
  • A-Tier (Strong & Reliable): Excellent weapons with a narrower advantage or a higher investment requirement.
  • B-Tier (Situational): Works when the map, hero, rank or loadout supplies the missing conditions.
  • C-Tier (Weak): Falls behind on the framework unless a specific account state makes it temporarily useful.
Wave control · 30%

How consistently the weapon clears lanes, groups and multi-direction pressure.

Boss pressure · 25%

How well it finishes elites and bosses without abandoning normal-wave coverage.

Scaling · 20%

How reliably damage, reach or utility improves as the weapon develops.

Safety · 15%

How much positioning freedom and survival margin the weapon provides.

Investment value · 10%

How efficiently current levels, ranks and upgrades translate into a usable slot.

S-Tier Weapons (Meta Defining)

S

Plasma Rifle / Beam

High Pierce AoE DPS

The undisputed king of the late game. The Plasma Beam fires massive laser columns that incinerate entire hordes in a straight line. Often cited by the community as "mandatory" for surviving past Wave 20.

Database Stats

Sword

High Knockback Scaling Damage

An incredibly reliable melee weapon that sweeps in front of the player. Upgrading the Sword increases its range and damage exponentially. It forms the backbone of most protective builds.

A-Tier Weapons (Strong & Reliable)

A

Axe

Orbital Defense

The Axe orbits your character, creating a barrier of damage. It is a fantastic defensive tool for blocking incoming enemies but its attack speed can lag against extremely dense, high-HP swarms compared to S-Tier options.

Database Stats

Chain Lightning

Bounce Mechanic

Excellent for clearing out scattered mobs during the early to mid-game. It jumps automatically between enemies, taking the pressure off your aiming. It only falls out of S-Tier because of lower raw boss damage.

Database Stats

Shotgun

Burst DPS

Deals massive burst damage at very close range. An exceptional tool for directly engaging Elite enemies or carving an escape path through a wall of mobs.

Database Stats

Wand / Magic Staff

High Frequency

Highly efficient, long-range magical attacks. It scales incredibly well with Attack Speed upgrades, turning into a rapid-fire magic machine gun late game.

B-Tier & C-Tier Weapons (Situational Picks)

B/C

Frostbite / Ice Dagger (B-Tier)

Deals inherently low damage, but applies a Slow/Freeze effect. It is a pure Crowd-Control utility weapon. It will not win the game alone, but is great when paired with Rocket Launchers.

Database Stats

Fists / Crossbow (C-Tier)

Very basic early-game tools. While decent at Wave 1, their lack of AoE scaling means they fall off rapidly. Use them only until you find something better in a chest or shop.

Should You Merge Lower Tier Weapons?

A common question is whether you should merge three C-Tier weapons into one higher-rarity tier. In Monster Survivors' merge system (combining 3 identical items to upgrade their color rarity), stat inflation often beats raw weapon tier.

A Legendary (Gold) Crossbow will deal significantly more damage than a Common (White) Plasma Gun. Always upgrade what you find, but transition to the S-Tier and A-Tier meta picks whenever you Secure Epic/Legendary versions of them.
