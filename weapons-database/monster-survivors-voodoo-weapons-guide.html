How to Read Weapon Damage Notes

Damage results depend on weapon level, available upgrades, hero choice, passive items, positioning and the current game version. The official store listing confirms the App has character progression and boss fights, but it does not publish a complete weapon damage table.

  • Crowd clear: check how consistently the build removes dense waves and preserves an escape route.
  • Boss damage: check damage during safe contact windows instead of relying on a static “best” label.
  • Evolution value: reserve the required component only when the route is confirmed in your current version.
