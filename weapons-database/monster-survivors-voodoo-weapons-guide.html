What are "Voodoo Weapons"?

If you are searching for **"Monster Survivors Voodoo Weapons,"** you are looking for the official weapon set in the mobile and web versions of the game published by Voodoo (developed by FreezeNova). There are no specific items named "Voodoo Weapons" in-game, but rather a collection of 15+ lethal tools that can be upgraded and evolved.

Understanding Weapon Rarity

In Monster Survivors, you upgrade weapons by merging three identical items or collecting XP crystals in-game. The power jump between tiers is exponential:

  • Common (Brown): 100% Damage base.
  • Rare (Blue): +50% Damage, +1 Projectile.
  • Epic (Purple): +100% Damage, unique passive unlocked.
  • Legendary (Gold): Maximum stats + Evolution potential.
