Plasma Gun / Beam

Widely regarded by the community as a "mandatory" weapon for deep runs. The Plasma Gun generates massive area-of-effect (AoE) damage that completely erases dense packs of enemies. At maximum rarity, it transforms into an unstoppable screen-clearing beam.

Mechanics: The Screen Erasing Beam

Like the Axe, the Plasma Gun does not fuse with an accessory to form a uniquely named evolution. Instead, it scales exponentially through Rarity and Supercharge points:

  • Infinite Penetration: At Legendary Rarity, the Plasma Gun projects a beam that travels an "essentially infinite" distance across the screen, damaging everything in its path simultaneously. It completely ignores enemy body-blocking.
  • Area of Effect: The beam grows incredibly thick, acting less like a gun and more like a massive wall of deletion.
  • The Trade-off: The Plasma beam has a noticeable firing interval/cooldown. It also suffers from weaker single-target DPS, making it highly inefficient at taking down elite bosses quickly.

Playstyle & Synergies

The Plasma Beam dictates your macro-movement strategy. Because it sweeps back and forth or fires in thick cones, you want to kite enemies into massive, clustered balls.

Best Hero: Echo

The community consensus heavily pairs the Plasma Gun with the hero Echo. Echo's innate Area Damage multiplier combined with the Plasma Gun creates a map-wiping synergy where the beam's width covers nearly 40% of the visible un-shrunk arena.

The "Perfect Storm" Weapon Pairing

Because the Plasma Gun struggles against Bosses, you **must** pair it with a single-target "Boss Buster". The most common highly-effective loadouts combine the Plasma Gun (AoE) with either the Fist (Stun/Impact) or the Sword (High base DPS). The Plasma clears the small fry so the Fist can isolate and crush the boss.

Warning: Developer Balance Nerfs

The Plasma Gun has historically sat at the absolute top of the meta. Consequently, it is a frequent target for developer community adjustments. Some players have noted recent "silent nerfs" to its damage output against elite mobs. Always hedge your bets by carrying a reliable secondary weapon.
