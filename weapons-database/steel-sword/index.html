Steel Sword / Short Sword

The definitive foundational melee weapon in Monster Survivors. Known interchangeably as the Steel Sword or Short Sword, this high-DPS blade slices directly in front of the player, punishing enemies that close the gap.

Weapon Evolution: The Holy Sword

+ =

Evolution Formula: Combine a max-level Short Sword with the Holy Cross to unlock the Holy Sword. This evolution transforms the basic melee slashes into massive holy waves that deal devastating cleave damage across the arena, resolving the base weapon's fatal flaw (its short range).

Playstyle & Hero Synergies

Because the Steel Sword attacks immediately in whichever direction you are moving, it requires a confident "hit-and-run" playstyle. You must actively thread the needle through enemy hordes rather than standing safely at a distance.

Best Hero: The Knight

The Knight is the undisputed best hero for this weapon, as the Short Sword is his dedicated starting gear. The Knight's innate armor and HP bonuses perfectly offset the inherent danger of fighting at melee range.

Stat Priorities

  • 1. Attack Speed Scaling the frequency of your swings turns the sword into a continuous chainsaw, preventing enemies from slipping between attack cooldowns.
  • 2. Critical Damage Because the base damage multiplier on the sword is inherently high, multiplying it further with Crit creates a build capable of one-shotting elites.
  • 3. Movement Speed Vital for safely engaging and disengaging from massive Boss hitboxes without taking contact damage.
