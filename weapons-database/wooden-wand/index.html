Wooden Wand (Magic Staff)

Weapon Evolution: The Thunder Staff

+ =

Evolution Formula: Combine a max-level Magic Staff (Wooden Wand) with the Lightning Ring passive accessory to unlock the Thunder Staff. This god-tier evolution replaces single projectiles with chain-lightning bolts that auto-jump between multiple enemies, eliminating the need to carefully aim your shots!

Playstyle & Hero Synergies

The Wand promotes a "safe kiting" methodology. You can walk in wide circles around the map perimeter, constantly firing into the horde trailing behind you. This makes it an incredibly safe weapon for pushing into later, unfamiliar waves.

Best Hero: Mage and Arcanix

The Mage starts directly with this staff and possesses innate Area Damage bonuses. Arcanix is also a flawless pairing, as his raw Magical Output turns even a level 1 Wand into a lethal railgun.

Stat Priorities

  • 1. Cooldown Reduction (Empty Tome) Vital. Dropping the cooldown ensures you're firing a continuous stream of projectiles, rather than bursts that allow fast enemies (like Bees) to slip through.
  • 2. Area of Effect (Candelabra) Increases the hitbox of the projectiles, allowing a single piercing shot to hit overlapping enemies at once.
  • 3. Raw Damage (Spinach) Because the base damage of the wand is lower than the Sword or Fist, scaling up the "Might" multiplier keeps it relevant against late-game Boss health pools.

The "Invincible Cloak" Strat

Top-tier players often build the Thunder Staff (for long-range swarm clear) and combine it with a maxed-out Sword/Axe as an immediate close-range panic button. The Axe grinds anything the lightning happens to miss, creating a near impenetrable dual-layered defense.
