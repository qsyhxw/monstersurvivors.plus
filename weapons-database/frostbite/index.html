Frostbite (Ice Dagger)

Frostbite, frequently referred to by the community as the Ice Dagger, is an advanced defensive weapon. It sacrifices raw burst damage in exchange for unparalleled crowd-control, freezing the game's most dangerous elite enemies in their tracks.

Mechanics: The Absolute Zero Engine

Frostbite separates itself from other magic weapons by treating damage as an afterthought. Its value is purely mathematical: it buys you time.

  • The Slowing Decay: Basic hits apply a slowing debuff to the target's movement speed. This effect stacks, meaning a steady barrage of ice daggers will eventually bring a fast-moving boss to a glacial crawl.
  • The Hard Freeze: At higher rarities or through specific Supercharge nodes, Frostbite can proc a "Hard Freeze." The enemy is completely immobilized and rendered incapable of dealing contact damage for a few precious seconds.
  • Scaling: Unlike weapons that evolve by combining with specific rings (e.g., Thunder Staff), Frostbite scales its utility strictly through leveling and rarity upgrades, increasing the duration of its crowd-control states.

How to Build the Ultimate Defense

Because Frostbite does very little damage on its own, you must treat it as a supportive "shield" weapon. Your passive item choices must focus on blanketing the screen in ice.

Vital Stat Priorities

  • 1. Cooldown Reduction The single most important stat. A low cooldown means you are reapplying the Slow/Freeze debuff before the previous one expires, essentially achieving "Stunlock" on difficult targets.
  • 2. Amount (Extra Projectiles) More daggers flying out ensures that you are freezing multiple incoming threats at once, rather than just one unlucky target.
  • 3. Raw Damage (Avoid) Do not waste passive items trying to increase Frostbite's damage. It has terrible base multipliers. Let your other weapons handle the killing.

The "Shatter" Synergy (Weapon Pairing)

Frostbite is universally ranked in the top 5 weapons precisely because it enables extreme high-damage, low-range weapons to function perfectly.

Frostbite + Shotgun

Freeze the Boss in place, allowing you to walk into point-blank range with the Shotgun and unload a full scatter-burst directly into its face with zero risk of taking contact damage.

Frostbite + Plasma Beam

Lock the rapidly moving swarms (like Bees) in a tightly frozen cluster, turning them into sitting ducks for the slow-firing, infinite-cleave Plasma Gun.
