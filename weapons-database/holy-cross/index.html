Holy Cross Role: Coverage and Damage Uptime

The current guide treats the Holy Cross as a rotating area weapon rather than a straight-line projectile. Its value comes from keeping coverage around the player while you move through a wave.

  • Crowd clear: Rotating coverage can help manage groups approaching from several directions; keep a movement lane open instead of standing still.
  • Boss damage: Damage depends on contact uptime, positioning and the current balance version. Treat the Cross as a coverage tool and pair it with a separate single-target answer when a boss timer is the problem.

Holy Cross Build Priorities: Size, Amount and Duration

For a Cross-centered build, prioritize choices that improve coverage and uptime. If you are taking the Cross mainly for the Holy Sword route, preserve a second damage lane and do not assume every Cross stat is the best next pick.

Vital Stat Priorities

  • 1. Area of Effect (Size) Use size to test wider coverage and a more forgiving movement path. Confirm how the current build applies the stat to the Cross and its orbit.
  • 2. Amount (Extra Projectiles) More projectiles can improve coverage, but the useful cap and upgrade availability are version-sensitive. Compare the result against cooldown and survivability choices.
  • 3. Duration and Cooldown Aim for reliable uptime rather than a fixed numeric breakpoint. Track whether gaps in coverage or weak boss damage is causing the failure before spending the next upgrade.

Caution: Check the Inner Gap

Orbit-based coverage can leave a close-range gap. If enemies get inside the rotation, keep moving and use a complementary damage or defensive option instead of assuming the Cross alone covers every angle.
