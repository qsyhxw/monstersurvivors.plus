Holy Book & Fire Wand

Often grouped together in "Turtle Builds," these two advanced weapons excel at Area Denial. Rather than hunting enemies, they punish enemies that dare to cross into your personal zone through physical barriers and persistent ground fire.

The Holy Book: The Iron Maiden

The Holy Book acts as a direct physical counter to swarm mechanics. It does not shoot projectiles outwards; rather, it creates a whirling Protective Barrier tightly hugging your character's hitbox.

  • Close-Quarters Knockback: When fast enemies (like hounds or bats) try to rush you, the rapidly spinning Holy Books will bat them away before they can inflict contact damage.
  • The Despawn Window: The barrier is not permanent. It spins for a set duration and then despawns, leaving you completely vulnerable while it is on cooldown. Building around it means minimizing this gap.

Fire Wand: Scorched Earth

While standard Wands act as direct-fire guns, the Fire Wand functions more like a trap-layer. It casts large zones of fire onto the ground that linger over time.

  • Damage Over Time (DoT): Enemies that walk into the fire zones are afflicted with a burning status, constantly taking tick damage for as long as they stand in the flames.
  • Kiting Synergy: The Fire Wand is the absolute best weapon to use when playing a coward's game. You cast the fire, and then slowly walk around it, forcing brainless melee mobs to path directly through the flames to reach you.

How to Build the "Turtle" Strategy

Combining these two weapons creates a powerful "Standing Still" or "Small Circle Kiting" strategy. Your passive items must support keeping these effects on the screen for as long as mathematically possible.

Vital Stat Priorities

  • 1. Duration & Cooldown Reduction The holiest of combinations. High Duration keeps the Holy Book spinning and the Fire patches burning longer. High Cooldown Reduction means new books/fire spawn before the old ones vanish. The goal is 100% constant uptime.
  • 2. Area of Effect (Size) Forces the Holy Book's barrier outward away from your body, giving you more breathing room. Also creates massive Fire patches that cover entire choke points.
  • 3. Armor / Health Regen Because Area Denial relies on letting enemies come to you rather than clearing the whole screen, you will inevitably take chip damage. Defensive stats are mandatory.

Caution: The Stand-Still Death Sentence

Never assume your Holy Book barrier is fully impenetrable. Endgame Elite enemies often have very high Knockback Resistance. If they charge your barrier, they will clip through the books and hit you. Always keep stutter-stepping around your Fire Wand patches rather than standing perfectly still.
