How Weapons & Upgrades Work

At the start of every run, you will select a primary weapon (often dictated by your Hero, such as the Knight's Short Sword or the Mage's Magic Staff). As you collect experience crystals from defeated enemies, you will level up and be offered a choice of random weapons and passive abilities.

Weapons improve exponentially with their rarity. As a weapon increases in level (stepping up to Legendary tier), its base damage, frequency of attack, and area of effect increase drastically. Furthermore, certain weapons have hidden synergies or evolutionary paths when combined during a run.

Melee Weapons

Melee / Close Range

Steel Sword / Short Sword

A traditional starting option. Slices directly in front of the player. Requires you to step into danger, but rewards you with high base damage and critical scaling. Excellent base component for evolutionary builds.

Great with: Attack Speed, Crit Damage
Melee / Barrier

Axe

Swirls actively around the player in a growing orbit. At legendary rarity, multiple axes rotate simultaneously, forming a virtually impenetrable shield against swarming minor enemies.

★ S-Tier Potential
Melee / Impact

Fist

A heavy, blunt collision weapon. Excellent at stunning elite enemies and bosses when they get uncomfortably close, providing brief but critical breathing room.

Role: Disruption / Boss Control

Magic Weapons

Magic / Projectile

Wooden Wand / Wand

The foundational magic weapon (and Arcanix's signature). Shoots guided or straight-line projectiles. At high rarity, its firing frequency and DPS scale enormously, making it one of the most reliable sources of constant damage.

Usage: Single-target & Safe Kiting
Magic / Area Damage

Plasma Gun / Beam

A mandatory choice for pushing late-game waves. The Plasma Gun generates massive area-of-effect damage, completely erasing dense packs of enemies and eating chunk damage from Bosses.

★ S-Tier Potential
Magic / Piercing

Magic Staff

A ranged weapon utilizing piercing magical bolts. It guarantees value against lines of enemies following the player, making it an excellent early-mid game clearer.

Synergy: Linear Kiting
Magic / Chain Effect

Chain Lightning & Ring

Strikes an enemy and automatically jumps to nearby foes. Unparalleled for early game crowd control when stats are too low to physically bash through mobs.

Role: Early Swarm Clearer

Ranged Physical Weapons

Ranged / Burst

Shotgun

Delivers immense burst damage directly in front of the player. Perfect for blasting open escape routes when backed into a corner by aggressive Crab or Pumpkin spawns.

Priority: Close-quarters emergency
Ranged / Area Push

Rocket Launcher

Creates large explosions upon impact. Excellent knockback properties against dense, slow-moving mobs, stalling them completely.

Priority: Heavy Knockback
Ranged / Pierce

Crossbow & Throwing Knife

Fast projectiles associated with high base critical-hit chances. Though somewhat weak in early rarities, they become machine-gun-like shredders against bosses once pushing past Legendary tier.

Great with: Critical Chance gear

Utility & Evolving Weapons

Utility / Status

Frostbite (Ice Dagger)

Trading raw damage for unparalleled crowd control, this weapon freezes aggressive enemies in their tracks. Essential for buying time and repositioning during chaotic late-game boss encounters.

Role: Hard Crowd Control
Utility / Evolution

Holy Cross (Holy Sword)

The Holy Cross provides fantastic rotational coverage around the map. Crucially, when strategically combined with base sword weapons, it allows permutations like the evolved Holy Sword.

Synergy: Backbone for evolved builds
Utility / Area Denial

Holy Book & Fire Wand

While the Holy Book generates a close-proximity physical protective barrier, the Fire Wand casts area denial zones (Damage over Time) on the ground, punishing enemies that chase you.

Usage: Area Denial / Safe Zones
