Shotgun

The Shotgun is a highly divisive spread-fire weapon. While it packs an unparalleled burst-damage punch at point-blank range against elite bosses, its devastatingly slow reload time leaves players dangerously vulnerable.

Mechanics: The "Point-Blank" Gimmick

Unlike the Magic Staff or Plasma Gun which scale to cover massive areas of the screen cleanly, the Shotgun relies purely on Scatter Physics.

  • Cone Width: The weapon fires multiple pellets in a wide cone. Targets at the edge of your screen will only get clipped by 1 or 2 pellets, resulting in miserable damage.
  • Point-Blank Chunking: To achieve acceptable DPS, you must walk dangerously close to an elite enemy. Forcing all pellets to collide with a single target creates a massive chunk of burst damage.
  • No Fusion Evolution: The Shotgun currently does not have a confirmed item-fusion evolution. It relies entirely on leveling up its base Rarity to increase pellet count and reduce reload time.

The Community Consensus: Proceed with Caution

In Reddit and community Tier Lists, the Shotgun is frequently ranked as a weapon to avoid. In a game where "Swarms" (massive numbers of small enemies) are the primary cause of death in late waves, using a slow-firing, low-pierce weapon almost guarantees you will get swarmed and pinned against a wall. It is generally recommended to pick the Fist (for stunlocks) or the Sword instead if you need a Boss Killer.

How to Build the Shotgun (If You Must)

If you happen to get a Legendary drop of the Shotgun or are trying to challenge yourself, you must build purely around mitigating its weaknesses.

Vital Stat Priorities

  • 1. Movement Speed Mandatory. You need to rapidly dive into point-blank range to deliver a burst to a Boss, and instantly sprint away while the agonizingly slow reload animation plays out.
  • 2. Cooldown Reduction Lowers the "pump" animation time between volleys, slightly increasing your margin for error if you miss a shot.
  • 3. Defensive Passives (Armor / Regen) Because you are playing at point-blank range without the Stunlock privileges of the Fist, taking chip damage is inevitable. You must build to survive the hits you are guaranteed to take.

Weapon Pairing

Never run the Shotgun as your primary clearer. Pair it with the Plasma Gun or Chain Lightning to keep the small trash mobs off you, reserving the Shotgun blasts strictly for cracking the shells of Bosses and Elites.

Meta Version Note: This guide reflects community sentiment and mechanics for the Shotgun in its current state. Because it is widely considered underpowered, there is a high probability FreezeNova will buff its base spread or reload speed in a future patch. Always check recent patch notes.
