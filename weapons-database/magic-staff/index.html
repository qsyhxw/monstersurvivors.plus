Magic Staff

A focused ranged weapon utilizing piercing magical bolts. Unlike the chain-tracking properties of other wands, the Magic Staff rewards precise positioning and linear kiting, making it an incredibly efficient early-mid game swarm clearer.

Weapon Evolution: The Archmage Staff

+ =

Evolution Path: Maxing out the Magic Staff allows it to merge into the fabled Archmage Staff. This advanced elemental form significantly increases the physical width of the piercing bolts and adds devastating elemental effects capable of shredding densely packed elite enemies instantly.

Playstyle & Linear Kiting

To get the maximum value out of the Magic Staff, you cannot simply run in circles. The piercing bolts only hit enemies traveling in a straight line behind you. This gives birth to the "Linear Kiting" maneuver: walking completely backward across the map so that all pursuing enemies file into a single, dense column. A single pierced shot will then damage all of them.

Vital Stat Priorities

  • 1. Projectile Speed Faster projectiles mean the piercing bolt travels through the enemy column before they can naturally spread out or adjust their pathing.
  • 2. Area / Hitbox Size Increasing the area makes your linear bolts "fatter," allowing you to hit multiple parallel columns of tracing enemies simultaneously.
  • 3. Attack Speed Increases the frequency of your bolts, effectively creating a permanent laser beam of death behind you as you move.

The Danger of Map Borders

Linear Kiting is an extremely potent strategy, but it requires vast open space. During Boss Battles, the arena shrinks dramatically. You must transition from straight-line kiting to tight circle kiting, which drastically reduces the piercing efficiency of this weapon. Always carry a secondary defensive weapon for tight spaces!
