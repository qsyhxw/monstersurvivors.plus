Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best weapon combination in Monster Survivors?

The absolute meta requires combining long-range crowd control with high DPS. The Sword and Plasma Beam, backed up by defensive orbiters like the Axe, is the consistently highest-rated combination for reaching wave 30+.

How do you evolve weapons in Monster Survivors?

You evolve weapons in-game by collecting XP gems and selecting the same weapon to upgrade until it hits Max Level. To permanently upgrade base rarity color (e.g. White to Green), you must merge 3 identical items in the main menu screen.

Who is the strongest hero to unlock?

Based on 2026 meta analysis, Echo and Arcanix are the strongest unlockable heroes. Echo provides an essential defensive armor layer, while Arcanix brings exceptional magical damage and crowd control.
