How the App Guides Connect

Before the run

  • Hero: choose the trait and risk profile.
  • Persistent weapons: invest in the roles used most often.
  • Gear: prioritize useful effects and keeper pieces.
  • Permanent upgrades: improve broad value or the current bottleneck.

During and after the run

  • Map: adjust coverage, focused damage, Speed or defense.
  • Skills: concentrate levels on the build's main job.
  • Failure review: record why the attempt ended.
  • Next spend: upgrade only the layer responsible.

Avoid wrong-version advice

Do Not Use These Signals for the App

FreezeNova or “Play Online” mechanics

That release has a separate progression loop and should stay inside the online-game section.

Wooden Wand, Steel Sword or Axe routes

The App weapon pages currently focus on Plasma Beam, Chain Lightning, Shotgun, Sniper, Pistol and its own roster.

Arcanix, Echo or browser map names

Use the hero cards and maps visible in the VOODOO App, such as Balancio and Frightful Foothills.

Old exact cost or merge claims

App values can change. The current upgrade, weapon and gear preview should decide the final action.

App Version Note

The official Google Play listing was updated June 12, 2026 and confirms character progression, unique skills, boss fights and rare loot. Weapon levels, hero effects, gear results and prices can change, so use the current in-game preview for exact values.

Monster Survivors App Guide FAQ

Where should a new App player start?

Start with Best Builds to choose one complete general setup. Then use Best Weapons to refine its roles and App Upgrades before spending scarce permanent resources.

Where are Gilded Cores and gear explained?

Use Gilded Cores for reward checks, farming and spending priorities. Use App Upgrades for the wider permanent-power and gear workflow.

Which guide should I use for a boss wall?

Use the Boss Hunter setup in Best Builds, then compare Sniper, Shotgun and the required crowd slot in Best Weapons.

Is Play Online the same game?

No. The App is published by VOODOO under package games.rivvy.monstersurvivors. Play Online is a separate browser game.
