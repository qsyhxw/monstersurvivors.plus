Phase 1: Progression & Milestones

These trophies will unlock naturally as you learn the game and buy upgrades. Do not stress about these during your first few runs.

First Blood

"Defeat 1,000 Monsters."

Solution: Unmissable. Simply play the game for about 10 minutes. The horde density increases significantly past Wave 3, guaranteeing this unlock early on.

True Survivor

"Reach Wave 15 in any arena."

Solution: This serves as the game's first major difficulty spike. Default heroes will struggle to survive the massive bullet-hell damage at Wave 15. The easiest way to cheese this trophy is to unlock the Knight hero and stack Armor.

Phase 2: Completionist / Grind Trophies

This is where the real 15+ hour grind begins. You will need exceptional gold farming routes and a deep knowledge of the game's weapon synergies.

Armed to the Teeth

"Unlock and use every single Base Weapon in the game at least once."

Solution: You must purchase all basic loadouts from the main menu and use them in a run. This requires roughly 50,000+ total gold. If you aren't sure which ones you are missing, compare your inventory to our full weapon encyclopedia.

The Hidden Truth

"Successfully forge a Secret Weapon Evolution."

Solution: The game does not explain how to do this. You must level a specific base weapon to Level 8, hold its perfect matching passive item, and then open a Boss Chest after Wave 10. You cannot guess these recipes; you must look them up.
See ALL Secret Evolution Recipes

DPS God

"Defeat the Final Boss in under 15 seconds."

Solution: Tanking will not work for this achievement. You are mathematically required to run a pure Crit-Damage loadout. The consensus strategy is using Miyu with evolved throwing knives or crossbows.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do web browser achievements transfer to mobile?

Generally, no. As a web HTML5 game, achievements are stored locally in your browser cache or tied to a web-specific profile. They do not automatically sync with Google Play Games or Apple Game Center unless the developer explicitly adds cloud saving in a future patch.
