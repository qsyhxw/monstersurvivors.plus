The 4 Best Weapons in Monster Survivors (Ranked)

Stop dying at Wave 20! Discover the absolute best weapons in Monster Survivors, from the screen-clearing Plasma Gun to the defensive Axe. Master the late-game meta today.

Hitting a brutal wall in the late waves of Monster Survivors? If you are constantly getting overwhelmed by fast-moving hordes or instantly deleted by elite bosses in Shade Palace, your movement strategy isn't the problem—your weapon choice is. The game actively rewards you for focusing on a single, overpowered weapon type and evolving it to Legendary tier.

Based on the 2026 meta and top player consensus on Reddit and community wikis, here is the definitive guide to the absolute best weapons in Monster Survivors that guarantee high-wave survival. (If you are completely new to the game, you may want to read our Best Starting Weapons Guide instead).

1. Plasma Gun (The Ultimate Swarm Eraser)

When you breach Wave 15, single-target weapons stop working. You need screen-clearing Area of Effect (AoE) damage, and absolutely nothing beats the Plasma Gun in the current meta.

  • Why it's the Best: Players routinely refer to the Plasma Gun (especially its green Legendary variant) as "mandatory" for late-game survival. It fires massive beams that incinerate dense packs of mobs instantly.
  • Playstyle: Use it as your primary damage dealer and kite enemies in large circles, allowing the wide beam to hit as many clustered targets as possible.

2. The Axe (The Impenetrable Shield)

You cannot outrun every enemy forever. Eventually, you will get cornered. The Axe is widely considered the best close-quarters and defensive weapon in the entire game.

  • Why it's the Best: Unlike swords that only attack forward, the Axe actively swirls in orbits around your character. Once upgraded to high rarity, multiple axes rotate simultaneously, forming a kinetic meat-grinder that acts as an impenetrable shield against weak swarms.
  • Best Synergy: The Arcanix Hero—combining her slowing aura with the Axe's orbit creates a permanent safe zone.
View Axe Stats in Database →

3. Magic Staff & Wand (The High-Frequency Shredder)

Do not underestimate the basic magical projectiles. Both the Wooden Wand and the Magic Staff scale incredibly well into the late game.

  • Why it's the Best: They boast massive firing frequency and piercing damage. The Magic Staff, in particular, can blast through straight lines of enemies, making it highly resource-efficient.
  • Pro Tip: If you are running the Wand, prioritize Attack Speed and Critical Chance upgrades to turn it into a magical machine gun.
View Wand Stats in Database →

4. Chain Lightning (The Early-Game Controller)

While it eventually falls off against ultra-tanky late-game bosses, Chain Lightning secures its spot on this list for carrying you effortlessly through the early to mid-game.

  • Why it's the Best: It automatically jumps between targets. During the first 10 minutes when your movement speed is low and you lack AoE from the Plasma Gun, this weapon ensures you don't take cheap chip-damage from loose goblins and slimes.
View Chain Lightning Stats in Database →

Honorable Mention: Frostbite (Ice Dagger)

Is the Ice Dagger the best damage weapon? No. In fact, its DPS is notoriously low. However, its status effect is undeniable. Frostbite freezes aggressive elites in their tracks, buying you the literal seconds you need to escape a fatal trap. Use it purely for utility and crowd control, not for killing.

High-Risk Warnings & Caveats

  • Frostbite (Ice Dagger) DPS Trap: As mentioned above, it is incredibly risky to invest solely into Frostbite upgrades under the assumption it will carry your damage. It is a pure support weapon. If you don't pair it with a heavy hitter like the Plasma Gun, you will lack the DPS to clear late-game waves and will eventually be overrun.
  • Plasma Gun Boss Weakness: While the Plasma Gun is the undisputed S-Tier weapon for clearing the screen of regular mobs, its single-target burst damage against Bosses is actually quite low. It is highly recommended to pair it with a Sword or Crossbow to deal with high-HP threats.
