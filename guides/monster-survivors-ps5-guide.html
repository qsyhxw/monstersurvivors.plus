OFFICIAL PS5 VERSION

Searching for Monster Survivors on PS5? You've come to the right place. While the mobile game is a global phenomenon, the PlayStation 5 version offers a unique, high-def roguelike experience. Here's everything you need to know about the PS5 release, trophies, and how it differs from the mobile version.

Important: Two Games, One Name

It is easy to get confused when searching for "Monster Survivors" on the PlayStation Store. Currently, there is a key distinction between versions:

The Mobile/Web Version

Typically published by Voodoo. Known for fast-paced waves and specific hero sets optimized for mobile play.

The PS5 Version

Developed by Two Llamas s.r.o. Specifically designed for consoles with trophy support and DualSense integration.

PS5 Quick FAQ

Can I play Monster Survivors for free on PS5?

No, the PS5 version is a paid digital title, though it is often priced very low (around $2.99 USD).

Is it cross-save with the mobile version?

Since the developers (Voodoo vs Two Llamas) are different, accounts and progress are not linked between platforms.
