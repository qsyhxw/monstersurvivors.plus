Monster Survivors Stats Guide

Stats are only good when they solve the next failed run. Use this page to choose between Damage, Attack Speed, Crit, Range, Move Speed, defense, Pickup Range, Luck and Gold without wasting upgrades or gear rolls.

See Stat Priority Upgrade Spending

Fast Answer

For most App accounts, start with broad combat stats, then add specialized stats only when the map or build asks for them.

  1. 1Damage + Attack Speed
    Best general pair for clearing, boss pressure and early upgrades.
  2. 2Range + Move Speed
    Add when you are trapped, kited poorly or miss targets.
  3. 3Crit + Luck + Gold
    Good after the build is safe, not before the build functions.

Use Current App Names and Values

The App may rename, rebalance or display stats differently across updates. This guide explains how to judge the stat role. When spending rare resources, confirm the exact value on your current upgrade, gear or reward screen.

EARLY ACCOUNT

Build the Core First

Damage and Attack Speed usually create the cleanest foundation because they help many weapons, heroes and maps.

PUSHING MAPS

Fix the Actual Death

If you die trapped, add Range, Move Speed or defense. If the boss survives, add focused damage instead.

FARMING

Add Economy Late

Pickup Range, Luck and Gold shine after the run is repeatable. They are weak emergency stats during a hard progression wall.

Build matching

Match Stats to Weapon and Hero Roles

A stat is stronger when it reinforces what the build already does. Do not chase a stat just because it looks rare.

Crowd-clear weapons

Often want Damage, Attack Speed and Range so the screen thins out before enemies touch you. See Best Weapons.

Boss-focused weapons

Need enough base Damage first, then Crit or focused scaling if the weapon hits often enough. See Weapon Combos.

Safe farming builds

Can carry Pickup Range, Luck or Gold after survival is solved. See Best Builds.

Keeper gear

Choose gear stats that support your main route before spending enhancement materials. See Best Gear Set.

Common Stat Mistakes

Taking Crit too early

Crit cannot save a build that lacks base damage, attack rate or weapon uptime.

Farming stats during a push

Luck, Gold and Pickup Range are attractive, but they should not replace the stat needed to clear the stage.

Stacking one stat only

Too much of one number can leave another bottleneck untouched. Balance kill speed, coverage and survival.

Ignoring the map

Open maps, cramped paths and boss-heavy stages change the value of Range, Move Speed and defense.

Stats FAQ

What stat should I upgrade first?

For most App accounts, start with Damage and Attack Speed because they help many heroes and weapons. Then add Range, Move Speed or defense when a specific map problem appears.

Is Luck worth it in Monster Survivors?

Luck is worth considering when your run is already safe and you are farming. It is usually a lower priority while you are stuck on a boss or dense wave.

Should I choose HP or Damage?

Choose HP or Defense if you die from unavoidable hits. Choose Damage if you survive long enough but cannot clear enemies or bosses before the run collapses.

Do stats change between the App and browser game?

Yes. This page is for the Monster Survivors App. The browser version on the Play Online page is separate and should not be used as the source for App upgrade decisions.
