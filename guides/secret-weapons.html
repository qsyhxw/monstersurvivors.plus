Looking for the cheat code to spawn an overpowered weapon? The truth is better than a cheat: The game hides its ultimate "Secret Weapons" in plain sight via Hidden Evolutions.

What are "Secret Weapons"?

When beginners ask about "Secret Weapons," they are actually looking for Weapon Evolutions. The game never explicitly tells you how to get them in the starting menus. To unlock a "Secret Weapon," you must upgrade a base weapon to its maximum level (Level 8) and hold its mathematically required Passive Item Synergy. Only then will the next Boss Chest drop the hidden, game-breaking final form.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are there cheat codes for secret weapons in Monster Survivors?

No, there are no traditional keyboard cheat codes to instantly spawn secret weapons. All ultimate weapons must be earned mid-run by executing the Hidden Evolutions mechanic (Level 8 Weapon + Specific Passive Item + Boss Chest).

What is the strongest hidden combination?

The strongest hidden synergy highly depends on your hero choice. However, as per our 2026 Tier List, evolving the Crossbow onto Miyu or forging the ultimate Magic Staff for Balencio yields the highest verifiable DPS outputs for Wave 15+.
