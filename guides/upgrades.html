Monster Survivors Upgrades Guide (2026): Best Priority & Gold Spending

Buy Damage I → Health I → Armor I for the strongest balanced start. Add Healing when recovery is the problem, and save Revive for runs that already reach the dangerous late section.

Best Gold Spending Route by Stage

A balanced route that buys cheap efficiency before 1,000- and 2,000-Gold tiers.

Stage 1: First 600 Gold

Spend: 600
  1. 1. Damage I
    200 Gold · 1.25× damage
  2. 2. Health I
    200 Gold · 100 value
  3. 3. Armor I
    200 Gold · 0.90× taken

This trio improves clear speed and effective durability immediately. If contact damage is not a problem but food recovery is, swap Armor I and Healing I.

Stage 2: Build a Stable Core

Next spend: 1,400

Buy Damage II, Health II, Armor II, and Healing I. Each second level costs 400 Gold; Healing I costs 200. At this point your stats cover all four repeatable failure points: slow kills, small health buffer, contact damage, and weak recovery.

Running total: 2,000 Gold from a fresh save.

Stage 3: Choose the 1,000-Gold Purchases

Mid-game decisions

Do not automatically buy all expensive tiers in menu order. Start with the upgrade that addresses the way your good runs end:

  • Damage III: best when enemy density overwhelms you.
  • Health III: best when a heavy hit ends a healthy run.
  • Armor III: best when repeated smaller hits drain you.
  • Revive: best when the run is already strong enough to continue after one death.

Stage 4: Finish the 2,000-Gold Tiers

Late progression

Max Damage first for faster clears, then Health or Armor based on whether burst hits or repeated contact is more dangerous. Max Healing last unless food recovery is central to your survival. Each final tier costs 2,000 Gold, so a targeted purchase is better than saving without a reason.

Common Gold-Wasting Mistakes

Saving 1,000 Gold before buying any stats

Three 200-Gold purchases give Damage I, Health I, and Armor I for only 600 Gold. That foundation usually helps more runs than rushing Revive from an unupgraded start.

Treating Health and Healing as the same upgrade

Health changes the size of the health pool; Healing changes food recovery. Buy the one connected to the actual problem.

Buying expensive tiers in menu order

At levels 3 and 4, every repeatable upgrade costs 1,000 or 2,000 Gold. Pick by failure point rather than keeping every row equal.

Following advice for a different release

The FreezeNova browser build uses Gold and the five upgrades listed here. A guide discussing Gilded Cores or a different upgrade menu may refer to another game or release.

Monster Survivors Upgrade FAQ

What is the best first upgrade?

Damage I is the best general first buy: it costs 200 Gold and sets the damage multiplier to 1.25×. Follow with Health I and Armor I for a balanced first 600 Gold. If survivability is clearly the only issue, Health I can come first without derailing the route.

How much Gold is needed to max everything?

Armor, Damage, Healing, and Health each cost 3,600 Gold to max: 200 + 400 + 1,000 + 2,000. Revive costs 1,000 Gold once. The full permanent-upgrade total is 15,400 Gold.

Should I buy Revive as soon as I reach 1,000 Gold?

Usually no. Revive is strongest when your run is already capable of reaching a dangerous late section. If weak damage or low durability causes early deaths, spend the same Gold on several lower-level core upgrades first.

Is Healing worth buying early?

Healing I is a good early-to-mid purchase for 200 Gold when food pickups are present but restore too little. It should not replace Health when a single hit can kill you before recovery becomes relevant.
