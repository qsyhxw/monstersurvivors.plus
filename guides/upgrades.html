Monster Survivors App Upgrades: Gilded Cores & Gear

Build a permanent foundation first, spend scarce Gilded Cores only on meaningful progression, and enhance equipment only after you know the piece is worth keeping.

Know what persists

The Three Upgrade Layers

The App has more than one long-term decision. Separate these layers before spending so a temporary gear boost does not consume resources needed for broader progression.

Simple rule: the broader the benefit and the harder it is to replace, the earlier it belongs in your upgrade order.

1. Permanent Progression

BUY BROAD VALUE FIRST

These gains carry into later attempts through account or survivor progression. Prioritize the benefit that helps your most-used hero and loadout across the widest range of stages.

  • Improves many future runs
  • Cannot be replaced by a random drop
  • Fixes clear speed or survival
  • Supports your main progression route

2. Gilded Core Spending

USE A SPENDING GATE

Treat Gilded Cores as a scarce progression resource, not a notification to clear immediately. Read the result preview and spend only when the gain is permanent, useful now and difficult to replace.

3. Gear Enhancement

ENHANCE KEEPERS

Equipment power is attached to a specific piece. That can be excellent value when the item fits your build, but poor value when the slot is about to be replaced. Protect useful duplicates and check every enhancement or merge preview.

When Should You Spend Gilded Cores?

Spend only when all three answers below are “yes.” If one answer is unclear, close the screen and keep the Cores.

1

Is the gain persistent?

Prefer a benefit that remains useful in later runs rather than a short-lived convenience or replaceable item.

2

Does it fix today's wall?

The upgrade should solve a real problem: slow clears, fragile runs, a useful unlock or a missing build function.

3

Is the result clear?

The current App preview should show what changes. Do not spend from memory of an older menu or another game version.

Spend now

  • The benefit helps your main hero or most-used build.
  • It unlocks a meaningful permanent effect or progression step.
  • The next stage repeatedly exposes the exact weakness it fixes.
  • You understand the cost and the result shown on screen.

Save instead

  • You are spending only to remove an upgrade notification.
  • The target is a temporary piece or unused secondary build.
  • The gain is marginal while a major unlock is within reach.
  • The guide you followed shows different menus, developers or currencies.

Equipment workflow

How to Strengthen Gear Without Regretting It

The safest route is selection first, enhancement second. Do not pour materials into every red dot in the inventory.

  1. 1

    Lock your current keepers

    Protect the pieces used by your main build and any strong alternate piece you may need later.

  2. 2

    Compare the effect, not only the number

    A higher score is not automatically better if the existing piece has a more useful effect for your hero, weapon or stage.

  3. 3

    Choose one bottleneck slot

    Improve the piece that directly increases damage, survival or build function instead of spreading materials evenly across every slot.

  4. 4

    Read the enhancement or merge preview

    Confirm the output piece, consumed items and displayed stat change before accepting. The current screen is the source of truth.

  5. 5

    Test before the next expensive step

    Run the stage that blocked you. If the upgrade solved the problem, keep building; if not, diagnose the next bottleneck before spending again.

Before You Use Gear as Material

  • It is not equipped by your main build.
  • It is not your best copy of a useful effect.
  • It is not reserved for a second hero or boss setup.
  • The result preview names the output you actually want.
  • You have checked whether the action can be reversed.

Stop if the preview is ambiguous. A missed stage is recoverable; consuming a valuable piece may not be.

Avoid wasted progress

Common Upgrade Mistakes

Using browser Gold advice in the App

The VOODOO App and the online browser game are separate products. Their currencies, menus and progression advice are not interchangeable.

Keeping every slot equally upgraded

Equal levels look tidy but often delay the one offensive or defensive upgrade that would actually move you forward.

Enhancing a piece because it is new

New rarity or a larger score does not guarantee better build value. Compare effects and replacement risk first.

Spending Cores without a target

A scarce resource should buy a clear outcome. Save it when you cannot explain how the purchase changes your next run.

Using every duplicate as fodder

A duplicate may support a later merge, another hero or a different boss setup. Protect useful effects before cleaning inventory.

Copying an old cost table

App rewards and prices can move between updates. Use the cost and result shown in your current upgrade screen.

Monster Survivors App Upgrade FAQ

What is the best first upgrade in the App?

Choose a permanent gain that helps most runs. Clear speed is the best general first target because removing enemies also reduces incoming pressure. Add enough survivability to stop one mistake from ending a good run, then invest more heavily in keeper gear.

Should I spend Gilded Cores as soon as I get them?

No. Spend when the preview shows a meaningful permanent benefit, useful unlock or direct answer to the wall blocking progress. Save when the gain is marginal, the target is replaceable or you are only reacting to a notification.

Should permanent upgrades or equipment come first?

Broad permanent value usually comes first because it helps more runs and cannot be replaced by a drop. Equipment becomes the better spend when the piece is a clear keeper and its effect directly supports your main loadout.

How do I avoid wasting gear materials?

Lock current keepers, preserve useful duplicates, compare effects instead of score alone and read the complete output preview before enhancing or merging. Stop if the consumed items or resulting piece are unclear.

Is this the same system as Play Online?

No. This page covers the VOODOO App. The site's Play Online page hosts a separate browser game, so its Gold upgrade order and costs should not be applied to Gilded Cores or App equipment.
