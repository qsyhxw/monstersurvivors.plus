Monster Survivors: Hunting Wilds Trophy Guide

Welcome to the absolute 100% achievement and trophy guide for the Hunting Wilds PlayStation expansion. Learn how to effortlessly secure all 11 trophies and melt the Void boss!

Roadmap & Overview

  • Estimated Trophy Difficulty: 3/10 (Varies heavily based on your kiting skills)
  • Approximate Time to 100%: 2 - 3 Hours
  • Offline Trophies: 11 (8 Gold, 1 Silver, 2 Bronze)
  • Missable Trophies: 0 (All stages can be replayed infinitely via the lobby menu)
  • Glitched Trophies: None reported in current patch.

Complete Trophy List

Into the Void Silver / Gold equivalent

Defeat the Void boss.

This is the ultimate challenge of the Hunting Wilds edition. The absolute safest method is to run a maxed Plasma Gun build to shred its health from afar while heavily dodging its grid lasers.

Slime Crusher

Defeat the Mega Slime boss.

Simply kite the Mega Slime in large circles. Do not get greedy when it charges its massive jump AoE attack; prioritize movement speed over raw DPS at this stage.

Shell Breaker & Wasp Queen Slayer (2 Trophies)

Defeat the Crab boss & Defeat the Queen Wasp boss.

For these specific bosses, piercing weapons like the Shotgun or the Crossbow are essential to clear out the annoying minions the Wasp Queen constantly summons in front of her.

Face the Mask & For Whom the Bell Tolls (2 Trophies)

Defeat the Mask boss & Defeat the Bell boss.

These mid-tier bosses rely heavily on telegraphed dash attacks. The trick is to circle-strafe them tightly so their dash always misses you by a few pixels.

New Ally Unlocked & New Grounds Discovered (2 Trophies)

Unlock the second playable character / Unlock the second stage.

These unlock automatically as part of natural story/stage progression. Just beat the first boss and head back to the lobby. You can check the Balencio guide if you want to master new character mechanics.

Upgrade Begins

Upgrade any ability for the first time.

Done in the main menu between runs. Use your hard-earned gold to purchase your first permanent stats boost. Very unmissable!

First Survival & Second Survival (2 Trophies)

Survive 9 minutes in the first stage / Survive 9 minutes in the second stage.

Your very first goal on any fresh run. Focus solely on staying alive rather than pushing out damage. Find a large open area and loop the massive horde in a circle. Check out the Beginner Survival Tricks section for an in-depth kiting breakdown.
