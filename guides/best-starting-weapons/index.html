Meta Selection Guide

Best Starting Weapon

Don't ruin your run at Wave 1. Your very first choice dictates your entire mid-game survival potential.

Why Your First Weapon Matters

In FreezeNova's Monster Survivors, the game algorithms heavily weight future treasure chests and level-up rolls based on your current inventory. If you pick a terrible weapon at Wave 1, the game will continually offer you upgrades for that terrible weapon, effectively bricking your progression. Below is the definitive ranking of the best starting weapons to pick—and which ones to avoid at all costs.

#1 S-TIER

The Magic Staff

The undisputed king of the early game. The Magic Staff fires a linear projectile that innately possesses the Pierce attribute. This means a single shot can pass through 3-4 weak starting mobs (like slime and goblins), allowing you to clear paths instantly.

Why it's best:

Pierces multiple enemies, immense early-game crowd control, transitions perfectly into late-game magic builds.

Best Hero Pairings:

Mage (+15% Area synergizes perfectly), Arcanix (stacks with Slow Aura).

#2 S-TIER

The Axe

If the Magic Staff is for offense, the Axe is the ultimate defensive starter. The Axe sweeps in an arc in front of your character, applying massive Knockback to anything it touches. It creates breathing room when you are getting surrounded by fast-moving early swelts.

Why it's best:

Highest base knockback, wide swing arc protects your flanks, very high base physical damage.

Best Hero Pairings:

Balencio (clears obstacles for parkour), Miyu (great hybrid foundation).

#3 A-TIER

The Short Sword

The Short Sword is a highly efficient single-target killer. It attacks extremely fast but has a very narrow hitbox facing forward. It is ranked slightly lower because it cannot hit multiple enemies effectively at Level 1, requiring you to actively aim your character's body at individual threats.

The Catch:

Incredible single-target DPS, but requires the Holy Cross drop later to evolve into the map-clearing Holy Sword.

Best Hero Pairings:

Echo (scales with his critical hits), Knight (default trusted weapon).

Weapons to Avoid (F-Tier)

AVOID

The Dagger / Throwing Knife

Never pick the Dagger as your first weapon. It targets the nearest enemy regardless of where you are facing, but its base damage is microscopic and it has zero knockback. You will be instantly overwhelmed by Wave 3 if you rely on this to secure early XP crystals.
