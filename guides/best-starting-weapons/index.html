Monster Survivors Best Starting Weapons

Choose the first weapon worth developing, keep useful existing investment and add the missing role before early resources are spread across too many cards.

This Guide Covers the VOODOO App

“Starting weapon” here means the first weapon an App account should develop into a reliable main option. Magic Staff, Axe, Short Sword and Dagger advice belongs to a different version and is not used on this page.

Quick answer

The Safest First Weapon Route

1

BEST TARGET

Plasma Beam

Make Plasma Beam the first broad progression target when it is available and close enough to your current weapon development to contribute. Its official description presents it as a weapon that sears through monsters.

2

LOWEST RESET RISK

Your Developed Bridge

Keep using the current high-level or high-rank weapon when a new target is still weak. Existing development matters more than replacing a useful card simply because another weapon ranks higher in general.

3

BOSS ANSWER

Sniper

Move Sniper up when ordinary enemies are manageable but a boss or durable target remains alive. Keep a broad weapon beside it so focused damage does not create a crowd-clear problem.

4

ACCOUNT-DEPENDENT

Shuriken or Blaster

Treat these as developed alternatives rather than automatic first choices. Use the current Level, Rank, visible effect and missing loadout role to decide whether either one should remain the bridge.

Safe early route

Build the First Weapon Core in Five Steps

  1. 1

    Read Every Current Card

    Compare Level, Rank, visible effects and the next preview. Do not compare names alone.

  2. 2

    Name the First Failure

    Separate crowd pressure, boss damage and unsafe positioning before choosing the investment.

  3. 3

    Choose One Main Project

    Use Plasma Beam as the broad target or keep the developed bridge until that switch is affordable.

  4. 4

    Add One Missing Role

    Add Sniper for focused damage or another current card that directly fixes the run's remaining weakness.

  5. 5

    Stop Before Resources Scatter

    Do not level every newly unlocked weapon. Stabilize the main pair, then use the full weapon guide to choose the flex role.

Spend decision

Switch Weapons or Upgrade the Current One?

Switch toward the new weapon if...

  • It fills a role the current loadout completely lacks.
  • Its present Level and Rank are close enough to contribute.
  • The next visible upgrade improves the main progression plan.
  • Replacing the current card does not remove the only crowd or boss answer.

Keep upgrading the current weapon if...

  • It has a meaningful development lead.
  • It is already solving the failure that matters.
  • The new option duplicates an existing role.
  • Switching would leave two weak projects instead of one reliable main weapon.

Protect early resources

Starting Weapon Mistakes

Following browser weapon advice

Magic Staff, Axe, Short Sword, Dagger and browser evolution recipes do not define the App's early weapon route.

Resetting for one ranking

A developed bridge can remain stronger and safer than a newly available general recommendation.

Developing four weapons equally

Early resources have more impact when one main weapon and one complementary role become reliable first.

Building only for bosses

Focused damage cannot help when ordinary enemies remove every safe lane before the boss matters.

Building only for crowds

A smooth wave can hide the lack of focused damage until a durable enemy stalls the run.

Ignoring the next preview

Check what the current App actually grants before committing materials to a card that may not fix the account.

Before spending

First Weapon Checklist

If several boxes remain unchecked, keep the materials and run the account comparison again.

  • I know whether crowds or bosses are the current wall.
  • I compared current Level and Rank.
  • I read the next visible upgrade.
  • The choice fills a real loadout role.
  • I am keeping a useful developed bridge.
  • I am not copying browser-game advice.
  • The main project remains affordable.
  • The second weapon complements the first.

FAQ

Monster Survivors Starting Weapon Questions

What is the best starting weapon in the App?

Plasma Beam is the safest broad early target when available and reasonably developed. Keep a stronger existing card as a bridge instead of forcing an immediate reset.

Should I upgrade Plasma Beam immediately?

Upgrade it when the current card and preview strengthen the main progression build. Delay deep spending if another card is much more developed or focused damage is the real wall.

When should Sniper become the priority?

Move Sniper up when waves are comfortable but a boss or durable target survives. Keep one broad weapon beside it.

Is the highest-rank current weapon always best?

Not always, but its development advantage deserves weight. Compare its role and next upgrade against the new option before switching.

Does this page rank every weapon?

No. This page solves the first-account investment decision. Use Best Weapons for the wider roster and Weapon Combos for complete packages.
