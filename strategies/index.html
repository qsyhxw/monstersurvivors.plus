Core Survival Strategies

These fundamental tactics form the foundation of successful Monster Survivors gameplay

Circle Kiting Movement

Move in large circular patterns around the battlefield. This keeps enemies trailing behind you in a predictable line, making them easier to manage while you collect XP gems.

Pro Tip: Never stand still. Constant movement is essential for survival.

Active XP Collection

Killing enemies does not automatically grant experience. You must physically collect the dropped XP gems by walking over them. Prioritize gem collection while staying mobile.

Warning: Ignoring gem collection will leave you underpowered against later waves.

Single Weapon Focus

When leveling up, prioritize upgrading one primary weapon before diversifying. A fully upgraded weapon is more effective than multiple weak ones in the early game.

Pro Tip: Choose upgrades that complement your starting weapon's playstyle.

Starting Weapon Strategies

Each starting weapon requires a different tactical approach

Steel Sword

Melee Combat Strategy

The Steel Sword excels at dealing high damage in close quarters. This weapon rewards aggressive players who can manage enemy positioning.

Recommended Tactics:

  • Use hit-and-run attacks: engage briefly, then retreat
  • Prioritize health and armor upgrades for survivability
  • Group enemies together before engaging for efficient damage

Best For: Players who enjoy aggressive, high-risk gameplay

Wooden Wand

Ranged Combat Strategy

The Wooden Wand allows you to attack enemies from a safe distance with magical projectiles. This weapon suits players who prefer tactical positioning.

Recommended Tactics:

  • Maintain distance and kite enemies in circular patterns
  • Focus on damage and attack speed upgrades
  • Use open space to your advantage before boss fights

Best For: Beginners and players who prefer safer, methodical gameplay

Boss Battle Strategies

When a boss appears, the battlefield shrinks. Here's how to survive these intense encounters

Boss Fight Mechanics

Understanding the shrinking arena

Boss battles in Monster Survivors are intense one-on-one confrontations. When a boss spawns, the play area contracts, forcing you into close combat with limited escape routes.

Do:

  • Keep moving in tight circles
  • Watch for attack patterns
  • Save health pickups for boss fights
  • Position near arena edges for escape routes

Don't:

  • Stand still even for a moment
  • Get cornered against the shrinking boundary
  • Panic and move erratically
  • Ignore boss spawn warnings

Key Insight: Defeating a boss returns you to the normal map size. The pressure is temporary - focus on survival first, damage second.

Permanent Upgrade Priority

Gold collected during runs can be spent on persistent upgrades in the main menu

Unlike in-run upgrades, these permanent stat boosts carry over between sessions. Invest your gold wisely to make each new run stronger from the start.

Available Permanent Upgrades:

Health

Increases maximum HP

Armor

Reduces incoming damage

Damage

Increases attack power

Healing

Improves HP recovery

Recommended Priority Order:

For beginners, consider prioritizing Health and Armor first for survivability. As you improve, shift focus to Damage for faster clears. Healing becomes more valuable in longer runs.
