Quick answer

Use a Specific Guide Instead of One Generic Strategy Page

If you are playing the VOODOO App, use App builds, weapons, upgrades, heroes and tier lists. If you are playing in a browser frame, use the browser play page and browser FAQ.

  • Do not copy old browser weapon or boss advice into App hero, gear or upgrade decisions.
  • Do not use a generic strategy article when your issue is a build, upgrade or version mismatch.
  • Use the version comparison when your screen, weapons or progression menus look different.
