Frequently Asked Questions

Is this game completely free?

Yes! Monster Survivors is completely free to play in your browser. No downloads, no registration, no hidden costs.

Does it work on mobile?

The browser version works best on desktop. For mobile, we recommend downloading the official Monster Survivors app from the App Store or Google Play.

Why is the game loading slowly?

Monster Survivors is a Unity game that needs to load assets. Initial loading may take 30-60 seconds depending on your connection. Once loaded, it runs smoothly.

Can I save my progress?

Progress in the browser version may be saved locally. For persistent progress across devices, download the official app versions.
