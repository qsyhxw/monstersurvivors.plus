Which Knight game are you playing?

This page concerns the Knight in the Monster Survivors browser game linked from our Play Online page. Knight Survivor is also the name of separate mobile games. Their weapon tiers, mythic items and upgrade rules cannot be inferred from this character guide.

Before following a build, match the game title, developer and weapon menu. For the VOODOO Monster Survivors App, use our App weapon recommendations. For a separately titled Knight Survivor app, check its own listing and in-game descriptions.

Choosing weapons for a browser Knight run

  1. Read your current starting weapon and passive card. We have not verified a universal speed percentage or starting sword across current releases.
  2. If your sword only reaches nearby enemies, leave an escape lane and compare an available ranged option before taking another close-range weapon.
  3. If crowds stop the run, compare coverage; if a boss survives, compare focused damage. Treat these as loadout decisions to try, rather than a measured weapon ranking.
  4. Read the current evolution preview before committing a slot. Short Sword plus Holy Cross producing Holy Sword is not verified here as a current recipe.

Fact check: October 29, 2026. Previous claims of a fixed +10% speed bonus, guaranteed Holy Sword evolution and one-shot critical hits have been withdrawn pending current gameplay evidence. This page does not supply a Knight Survivor mobile weapon tier list.
