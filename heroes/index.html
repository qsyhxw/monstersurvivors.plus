How to Unlock & Upgrade Heroes

In FreezeNova's Monster Survivors, you don't start with access to the entire roster. Heroes are unlocked primarily by collecting Hero Shards or spending in-game currency earned by completing runs and surviving monster waves.

Once unlocked, characters have persistent upgrades that carry over between game sessions. You can permanently upgrade their base armor, healing rates, damage output, and maximum health by investing collected resources in the central hub menu. Upgrading an S-Tier hero ensures you can push far beyond wave 20.
